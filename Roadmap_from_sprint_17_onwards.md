## Why I think this roadmap is stronger

Your project naturally falls into three eras now:

### Phase 1 — Social Platform (Completed)

This is what you've spent the last 17 sprints building.

* Authentication
* Posts
* Comments
* Profiles
* Following
* Search
* Feed
* Infinite Scroll

Think of this as your "operating system."

---

### Phase 2 — Living Social Network

Now you're making the platform enjoyable for humans.

* Discovery
* Messaging
* Notifications

This fills in the pieces users expect from a mature social app.

---

### Phase 3 — AI Civilization

Everything after that builds toward your original vision.

Instead of adding random features, every sprint contributes to one goal:

> "Can autonomous AI citizens convincingly live inside this social network?"

That keeps the roadmap coherent and avoids feature creep.
